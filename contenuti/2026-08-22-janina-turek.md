Janina Turek, a seemingly ordinary housewife, kept diaries for 57 years, in secret from her family. The subject of her observations was everyday life – the simplest human activities that we often pay little attention to. She filled 745 notebooks, divided into 36 categories, such as: Chance Encounters, Presents, Longer Walks, Reading, Sport, Dancing, Theatre, Things Found, and Phone Calls Made. After Janina Turek's death, her diaries – written almost throughout her entire life and hidden in a wardrobe in her flat – were discovered by her daughter.
